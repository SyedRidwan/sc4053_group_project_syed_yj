## v1 hidden bugs (scratch probes against v1 with only the price fix applied)

The original tests could not see these bugs. Each probe ran with 1,000 NTU, a start price of 0.1 ETH and a reserve of 0.02 ETH.

| Bug | Probe | Observed | Expected |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Double refund | Alice bids 48 ETH at t = 300 s; Bob bids 25 ETH at t = 900 s, then claims | Bob's net ETH −7; seller left with 31 ETH | Bob −16; seller 40 ETH |
| Seller drain | Seller calls `withdrawSellerProceeds` before Alice claims | Contract left with 0 ETH; Alice's claim reverts "ETH transfer failed" | Alice receives 600 NTU + 24 ETH |
| Unfunded start | Bid on an auction holding 0 NTU | Bid accepted | Bid rejected |
| Reentrancy | Test 4 attacker re-enters `claimTokensAndRefund` once | Attacker ends with 8 ETH + 200 NTU | 4 ETH + 100 NTU |

## v2 mutation testing

Each mutation reintroduces one bug into `DutchAuction.sol`, and the suite is run against it. A mutation "survives" if every test still passes.

| # | Mutation | Tests failing | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | Divide before multiply in `currentPrice` (v1 drift) | 7 | Killed |
| M2 | "Burn" by transfer to `0xdead` (supply unchanged) | 12 | Killed |
| M3 | Remove the `proceedsWithdrawn` check | 1 | Killed |
| M4 | Remove `claimed[msg.sender] = true` | 2 | Killed |
| M5 | Remove `nonReentrant` from `claim` | 1 | Killed |
| M6 | Record full `msg.value` instead of accepted ETH (v1 double refund) | 4 | Killed |
| M7 | `tokensOf = commitment / clearingPrice` instead of a pro-rata share | 0 at first, then 1 | Survived at first; killed after adding the rounding test |
| M8 | No cap on the bridging bid | 5 | Killed |
| M9 | Clearing price = current price instead of capital ÷ supply | 1 | Killed |

M7 is logged as AI-1 in `../ai-issues.md`. The AI's first v2 prototype used exactly this formula. When the auction sells out by the clock, the clearing price rounds down, and the claims add up to more than the contract holds. In the test, the last claimer was 2,263 token-wei short and their claim reverted (`ERC20InsufficientBalance`). 28 green tests did not catch it; mutation testing did.

## v2 Slither triage (20 results)

| Detector | Where | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `reentrancy-eth`, `reentrancy-benign` | `ReentrancyAttacker.receive()` | Intended: test-only attacker |
| `reentrancy-benign` | `DutchAuction.claim()` (auto-finalize burns, then writes `claimed`) | Accepted: the external call is `burn` on our own token, and `claim` is `nonReentrant` |
| `incorrect-equality` | `accepted == room`, `startTime == 0`, `totalCommitted == 0` | False positive: these compare tracked state, not `address(this).balance` |
| `timestamp` | Price and window checks | Accepted: the brief requires a time-bound auction; miners can shift timestamps by a few seconds, and each second of skew moves the price by about 0.0000667 ETH per token (0.08 ETH ÷ 1200 s) |
| `low-level-calls` | ETH refund and seller withdrawal | Required to send ETH; return values are checked and both paths are `nonReentrant` |
| `missing-inheritance` | `IDutchAuction` in the attacker | False positive: test interface |
| `naming-convention` | `ReentrancyAttacker._reject` | Cosmetic; test-only |
