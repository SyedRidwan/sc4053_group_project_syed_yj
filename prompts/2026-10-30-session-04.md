## Prompt 1
> I keep running into "transaction failed" even after clearing activity and nonce data. I clicked on claim tokens and "add TIM to wallet" but it keeps showing "transaction failed" even though i can see my TIMS after a minute or so

(With a screenshot of MetaMask's toast beside the app.)

**What the AI did:** read the chain directly: every transaction had status success, so the toast did not match reality.
It ran a workflow of three agents: one read the installed MetaMask program files (never wallet data), one reproduced
RPC behaviour on a throwaway chain, and one tried to refute the result. The conclusion, at about 75% confidence, was
that MetaMask's nonce-taken check marks a transaction "dropped" when an old confirmed record from before a chain
restart has the same nonce, and that it shows "dropped" as "Transaction failed". There is no dapp bug. The fix is to
clear activity after each restart, not before it. Logged as AI-18 and AI-19.
